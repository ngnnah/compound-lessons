## Design specificity
Authored for this product: patterns drawn from theme tokens and redrawn on theme change, rating dots that read as stamped seals, a vermilion rule under each takeaway. The skeleton under it (pill tabs, pill day strip, auto-fit grid) is generic, and the investing parts (price block, "Your rule") are the least designed.

Detector: CLI found 1 hit, `overused-font` (Instrument Serif), a false positive since the font is part of the identity; `cream-palette` (washi paper) is the same. The overlay ran headed on 8 views (6 desktop, 2 mobile). It agrees with the review on contrast: `--muted` #6E7383 is under 4.5:1 on every surface (4.49 on card, 4.1 on paper, 3.8 on the perf tint), and vermilion text and button labels land at 4.3. It caught two things the review missed: day-strip numerals at 10.9px (index.html:70) and unstyled placeholders at 4.0:1.

## What works
- Both themes are first-class because the patterns come from tokens.
- The lesson card reads well on a phone: serif title, short paragraphs, dot bullets, ruled takeaway. Highlights and threads are easy to tell apart.
- Missing capabilities degrade in the open, with a banner and a disabled button.

## Priority issues
1. [P1] The rating row breaks on a phone. `.dot` has no `flex-shrink:0` (index.html:226), so 32px dots squeeze into 20–26px ovals, and "Note 1" / "Ask 1" wrap to two lines. Fix: `flex-shrink:0`, Note and Ask on their own row, 44px targets on phones. Command: adapt.
2. [P1] "Not financial advice" is missing where the buy rule shows. Every lesson card shows a price and "Your rule: $X"; the disclaimer only lives in the Watchlist price note (index.html:959), which doesn't render before a snapshot exists. PRODUCT.md makes this a brand commitment. Fix: one line in `.perf`, plus the rule's arithmetic beside the amount. Command: clarify.
3. [P2] Muted text, vermilion labels and placeholders fail contrast. Fix: darken light-mode `--muted` and the vermilion used for text and button labels; style `::placeholder`. Command: polish.
4. [P2] The record can be lost in one tap. Delete thread, delete note and the ticker × have no undo, and "Ask 1" hides the existing thread. Breaks Principle 4. Fix: an undo toast on every delete; Ask opens the latest thread. Command: harden.
5. [P2] On a phone the first lesson starts ~650px down an 844px screen. Hero (311px), strip, then a vermilion "Show 2 more lessons" come first; the Notes tab is clipped with a hidden scrollbar. Fix: compact hero on phones; keep "Show 2 more" at the top (a CLAUDE.md convention) but style it as secondary, not vermilion. Command: layout.
